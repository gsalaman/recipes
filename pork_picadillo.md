# Pork Picadillo filling and sauce:
(Reduced from bayless filling for rellanos)

Vegetable oil
1 white onion
1 28-oz can crushed tomatoes
1/2 tsp cinnamon
1/2 tsp black pepper
1 cup chicken broth

1/3 cup almonds
1/3 cup raisins
1 lb ground pork
2 tsp cider vinegar

Heat 1.5 TBSP veggie oil in large saucepan over medium heat.
Chop onion into 1/4” pieces.
Brown in oil…10 min?
When well browned, raise heat to medium high and add tomatoes, cinnamon, and pepper.
Stir…want consistency of thick tomato sauce.  (May not take anything if you are doing crushed tomatoes.)
Remove 1 cup of this mixture for pork filling.  Add 1 cup broth.  Partially cover, simmer for about 45 min….probably the time you need to make the pork below.

Put 12” skillet over medium-high heat.  Add almonds and toast until deep golden (2 min).  Remove.
Add pork and fry for 10-15 min.
If there’s a lot of fat, drain it off.
Add cup of reserved tomato mix, vinegar, and raisins.  Cook over medium heat, stirring regularly until mixture is homogeneous (20 min?)
Add almonds, and add 1/2 tsp salt.
