# Vietnamese Style pork

I like using tenderloin...but you can just use std loin.  It's just a little tougher, so make sure you slice it thin!

Marinade for 1.5 lbs pork:
* 3 tablespoons shallots, minced
* 1.5 tablespoons minced garlic (2 heaping spoonfuls?)
* 1/4 cup sugar
* 1 tablespoon fish sauce 
* 1/2 tablespoon THICK soy sauce (? I usually use just my std low sodium)
* 1/2 tablespoon pepper
* 3 tablespoons oil

Then, the 8/29/2026 mod:  add 2 TBSP Hoisin!  Gives even more sweet umami!

Cut the pork into SMALL slices...think 1/4" or smaller.  Then pound it.  The thinner you go, the better it grills!

Do grill on full.  5 min/side usually does Alli-doneness...4 min/side is Glenn-doneness.
