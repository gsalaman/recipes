# Nuoc Chum 

This is my fav for the viet-pork!

* Juice of 1 lemon
* 2 tablespoons sugar
* 1/2 cup water
* 2 1/2 tbsp fish sauce
* 1 heaping spoonfull garlic.

Can add 1 or 2 thai chilies, or 1 tsp homemade chili garlic sauce.  Get it from Erika!!!

Combine lime, sugar, and water.  Taste it…does it have the right balance of sweet and sour?

Finish with fish sauce & garlic…taste again.  We’re going for a bold, forward finish — perhaps a little stronger than what you’d like.  Going for a light honey or amber.
